# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective patients researching and evaluating a primary care clinic before booking a first
appointment. They're comparing options, checking credibility, and looking for an easy next step
(book / contact) once reassured.

## Product Purpose

A landing page that showcases a primary/general care clinic's services and builds enough trust
for a visitor to take the next step toward booking an appointment.

## Positioning

Not established — no confirmed differentiator against other primary care clinics. Treat as an
open decision; don't invent a competitive claim.

## Operating Context

Visitor arrives via search or referral, scans the page to judge credibility and service fit, and
either books/contacts or leaves. No account system or patient portal in scope.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a **demo/testing project** built to exercise this workspace's Claude Code skill setup
  (`nextjs-vercel-skills`, shadcn/ui, Impeccable) end-to-end — not a real clinic's production
  site. Content is illustrative, not a real business's marketing copy.
- **Clinic name is a placeholder**, chosen for this exercise, not user-confirmed:
  "Meridian Family Care." Treat as inferred/placeholder — replace if this ever becomes real.
- No booking/backend system exists yet — CTAs are illustrative UI only, not wired to a real
  scheduling flow (matches `api/` being unscaffolded per `CLAUDE.md`).

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed. No real logo, voice guide, or identity constraints exist — later work should
treat name/voice as still-placeholder rather than a locked brand.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real testimonials, credentials, or case studies exist for this exercise — any shown on
the page must be clearly illustrative/placeholder, never presented as verified real evidence.

## Product Principles

- Approachable and trustworthy over clinical/cold — the primary job is lowering hesitation to
  book, not demonstrating medical authority alone.
- General/primary care framing — broad service list, not a single-specialty pitch.
- Low-friction path to the primary CTA (book/contact) from anywhere on the page.
- Content is illustrative for a skill/design-workflow exercise — never dressed up to look like a
  verified real clinic's claims (no fabricated licensing, real testimonials, or benchmarks).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific requirement beyond this workspace's standing rule: every visual change gets
a WCAG contrast/layout check via `/qa-visual` before being called done.
